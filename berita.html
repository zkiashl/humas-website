Stay Connected

IT FEST 6.0 is a technology festival organized by the Informatics Student Association (HIMTI) and the Informatics Engineering Study Program of Universitas Paramadina. Running from 27 July to 14 October 2026 under the theme “Human-Centered AI: Transforming the World with Integrity,” the festival brought together students, creators, and technology enthusiasts through competitions, a talkshow, an expo, a fun game, and a bazaar.

“ Ride the wave of creativity. From the Hackathon, Internet of Things, and Scientific Writing competitions to the talkshow, expo, fun game, and bazaar, IT FEST 6.0 gave everyone a stage to learn, build, and share ideas that put people first. Technology moves fast, but it should always move with integrity. ”

— IT FEST 6.0
